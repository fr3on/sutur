Hands: describe simple, clear hand positions (resting on a table or a knee, hanging at the sides, holding one object, in pockets, behind the back). Avoid fine finger actions and two people touching hands.
Held objects (guns, knives, telescopes, binoculars, razors, cups, letters) are allowed and should look right: one hand holds the object, natural anatomy with five fingers, arm lowered or to the side, the object clear and in proportion, seen from the side in a medium shot. A weapon is never aimed at anyone's head or face (aim it at the floor or away). The picture hint is added automatically; just describe the action simply.
Faces: at most two people in a close picture; groups only in wide shots. Show people from the side, from behind, or in medium shots when the action is hard to draw.
Never mention modern objects, not even to forbid them (phones, cars, cameras, bulbs). Name only objects of the story's period.
Avoid eyeglasses, mirrors, clocks with readable faces, maps and books with visible writing.
Show talk with posture and facing, never with the words talk, say, ask, answer, whisper, shout, or any bubble.
Violence and death stay implied: shadows, a closed door, a covered shape, an empty room.
Review fixes from the owner (append new ones below, one per line):
Interior scenes: when the text says a character waits at a door of their room, draw the inside of the room (furniture, candles), never a street.

Animals (a fish, dog, bird, lion) are real animals: describe them as the animal itself, with fins, tail or fur, never with a human head, body, clothes, hands or standing upright. A fish lives in water or lies on a plate; never put it in a cage.

Nudity only where the passage itself describes undressing, bathing or a bare body, and then non-explicit: wide shot, seen from behind, in steam or water, covered by hair, cloth or shadow; never sexual, never a child, and never a nude figure when the passage does not call for it. Everyone else is clothed in period clothing.
